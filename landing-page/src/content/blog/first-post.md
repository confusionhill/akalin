# Welcome to Akalin

Developing LLM-powered applications involves writing complex system prompts. However, when you change a system prompt to fix an edge case, how do you ensure you didn't regress on other behaviors?

Akalin is designed to solve exactly this problem. 

## Why We Built Akalin

We realized that "vibes-based" evaluation is not scalable. Relying on manual spot-checking to verify if a prompt behaves as expected leaves too much room for error, especially as prompts grow in complexity. 

With Akalin, you can:
- **Version Prompts**: Understand exactly what changed and when.
- **Define Custom Rubrics**: Move beyond binary pass/fail and grade responses on nuanced criteria (e.g. tone, formatting).
- **Use Any Model**: With the Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) feature, you are not locked into a single provider.

We are excited to see how the community uses Akalin to build robust, predictable AI features.
